Not sure which? Either address reaches me — pick whichever is closer.

If you're at school, please have an adult send it — a parent, teacher or coach. This site is built around student work, and I've set it up so it never collects anything about the students themselves: no accounts, no sign-ups, no email addresses kept. Keeping email to adults is part of that. If you're an IQ or V5 competitor, ask your coach to write on your behalf, or catch me in person at a competition.

VEX U teams can write directly. You're at university, so none of the above applies to you.

What to write about

  • Taking something down. If you or your team would rather a robot, a notebook or a photo weren't here, say so and it comes down. You don't have to give a reason, and I won't ask for one.
  • Something's wrong. A team number, a season, an award, a name spelled badly. Corrections are welcome and quick.
  • A rejected submission. If it was turned down and the reason doesn't make sense, or you're not sure how to fix it.
  • Adding an older robot. If you have CAD or photos of a robot from a past season that isn't here yet, that's exactly what this archive is short of.

What not to send

Don't email photos of students, or lists of names. The archive doesn't publish student faces, and I'd rather not receive them in the first place. Submit robots through the submission form instead.

Response time

This is a side project, run by one person, so it may take a few days. Takedown requests jump the queue — those get handled the same day I see them.

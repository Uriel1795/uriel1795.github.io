Send in your robot and it goes on the archive — a page of its own, with the 3D model, your notebook and whatever else you want kept. It's reviewed before it appears, and nothing publishes until after Worlds unless you've already shown the robot yourself.

Submissions are closed at the moment. If you'd like to send in a robot, get in touch and I'll sort it out directly.

← Robot Archive Compress

A robot straight out of CAD is usually far too big to put on a website — often hundreds of megabytes. This shrinks it, normally by 50× or more, without losing anything you can actually see. It takes about a minute.

Expect roughly 10× to 30× smaller. If you need the very smallest file, the desktop script in the archive's tools squeezes about three times harder — but this is easier, and the result loads fine either way.
